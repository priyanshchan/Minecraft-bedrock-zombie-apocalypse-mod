# Weapon attachments

Generated by `tools/gen/content.py` from `tools/gen/attach.py`. Fit attachments with the **Gunsmith** (Field Radio > Gunsmith, or use an attachment item while it is in your inventory).

## Effects

Numbers multiply the gun's own value (0.7 = 30% less). `spread`: hip-fire spread; `ads`: spread while crouched (aiming); `vrec` / `hrec`: vertical / horizontal recoil; `velocity`: muzzle velocity (flatter and longer flight); `noise`: how loud; `reload`: reload time; `mag`: magazine size; `mag_add`: extra rounds; `zoom`: magnification while aiming; `nv`: night vision while aiming; `laser`: a laser dot; `light`: lights up where you aim; `flash`: hides the muzzle flash; `bipod`: much steadier when crouched.

## How a fitted attachment shows on the gun

- Every attachment is modelled onto every gun it fits, in a bone `a_<id>` under the gun's `att_<slot>` bone (under `mag` for magazines), placed from the gun's mount.
- Each attachment item has a cooldown category `frontline_att_<id>`. While a gun with attachments is in hand, the scripts keep the categories of its fitted attachments running.
- The gun's render controller (`controller.render.frontline.gun_<gun>`) shows `a_<id>` while `q.cooldown_time_remaining('frontline_att_<id>') > 0`, and hides the factory part `def_<slot>` while any attachment of that slot shows.
- Cooldowns are the holder's own, so the attachments are drawn for the player holding the gun.

## Mounts per gun

Positions are in the gun's own frame and units (quarter pixels): `f` forward along the barrel, `u` up, `s` to the gun's left; `length` is the usable rail length.
